# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

RustForge-MLX is an open-source project built by and for people who want to learn, build, experiment, and contribute to machine learning and systems software in Rust.

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in the project.

Contributors should be able to participate without harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.

---

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

* Treat others with respect and professionalism.
* Communicate constructively and in good faith.
* Welcome different perspectives and levels of experience.
* Give and receive technical feedback respectfully.
* Focus criticism on ideas, code, designs, and decisions rather than individuals.
* Help maintain a productive technical environment.
* Respect project maintainers, contributors, and community members.
* Assume good intentions while addressing disagreements directly and constructively.

It is completely acceptable to disagree about an implementation, API, mathematical approach, or architectural decision.

Technical disagreement should remain focused on the problem rather than becoming personal.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment or discrimination.
* Personal attacks or insults.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Deliberate humiliation of other participants.
* Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
* Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Sustained unwanted contact or communication.
* Deliberately derailing technical discussions.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

---

## Technical Discussions

RustForge-MLX is a technical project, and strong technical disagreement is expected.

When discussing an implementation or proposal:

* Explain the reasoning behind your position.
* Provide evidence or examples where useful.
* Critique the implementation rather than the person.
* Be open to changing your position when new information becomes available.
* Prefer concrete technical discussion over speculation or personal judgment.

For example:

> "This implementation may produce incorrect results for singular matrices because the pivot is not checked."

is constructive.

Comments such as:

> "You clearly don't understand linear algebra."

are not.

---

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers through the project's available private communication channels.

When making a report, provide as much relevant information as you are comfortable sharing, including:

* What happened
* When it happened
* Where it happened
* Who was involved
* Any relevant links, messages, or other context

Please do not publicly repost private information or sensitive details when reporting an incident.

Reports will be handled as respectfully and confidentially as reasonably possible.

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for helping preserve a healthy project environment.

Maintainers may:

* Remove or edit content that violates this Code of Conduct.
* Close discussions that become abusive or disruptive.
* Temporarily restrict participation when necessary.
* Take other reasonable actions to protect contributors and maintain a productive community.

Maintainer decisions should be made fairly and consistently with the goals of this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including:

* GitHub issues
* Pull requests
* Discussions
* Code reviews
* Project-related chat or communication channels
* Other spaces where an individual is representing or participating in the RustForge-MLX community

The same standards apply whether participation is in person or online.

---

## Enforcement

Violations may result in actions including, depending on the circumstances:

1. A private or public warning.
2. Removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restriction from project spaces.
4. Permanent removal from project spaces.

The response will depend on the severity and context of the violation.

---

## No Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

Reports should not be used maliciously or intentionally to target other participants.

---

## Questions

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct or how it applies to a particular situation, contact the project maintainers through the project's normal communication channels.
